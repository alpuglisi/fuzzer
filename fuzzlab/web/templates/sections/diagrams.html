    How to read these diagrams
    • Observed: directly supported by captured traffic, a browser event, a tool exchange or explicit configuration. Inferred: the basis is shown. Unknown: not established.
    • No server-side component, data store or internal call is ever drawn: traffic shows that a browser talked to an origin, not what runs behind it.
    • Coverage counts what happened to an interface: discovered, captured, scheduled, executed, evaluated, finding. No finding is not "secure".
    • In sequences, a missing response is drawn lost (✕), and a finding is a tool record, never a target response.
